Before You Acquire

The Executive uses passive NFC technology — it draws power from the reader and requires no charging whatsoever. The Prestige includes a small rechargeable cell to power its ECG and health sensors, with a full charge lasting several days of continuous monitoring.

The Executive ring supports contactless payments at NFC-enabled terminals. Payment network compatibility (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) depends on the app and your issuing bank's support. Compatible with iOS and Android companion apps.

AxonRing is available in US sizes 6 through 12. A complimentary sizing guide is included with every order. International size conversion references are available upon request.

The Executive carries an IP67 rating and the Prestige an IP68 rating — both fully resistant to water exposure for swimming, showering, and everyday wear. AxonRing is designed to accompany you everywhere.

We offer a 30-day unconditional return policy. Executive editions carry a 1-year warranty; Prestige editions carry 2 years. All returns are collected free of charge from your address.

The Prestige ring pairs with a companion app that keeps your health data local to your device by default. NFC data on the Executive is stored on the ring's encrypted chip. No personal data is shared with third parties without your explicit consent.
