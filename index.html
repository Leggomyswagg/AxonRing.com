How It Works

Tap. Read.
Done.

1

Program it once

Use a free NFC app (NFC Tools on iOS/Android) to write your contact card, a link, or an automation trigger to the ring. Takes about five minutes; full guide included.

2

Tap any NFC phone

Hold the ring near the top (iPhone) or back (Android) of a phone. Their phone shows your contact or opens your link — no app needed on their side.

3

Rewrite anytime

New job? New link? Erase and rewrite the ring as often as you like. The chip is rated for years of rewrites.
