FAQ

Before you buy

Can it make contactless payments at stores?

No. It has no secure payment element. It can hold a payment link (Venmo, PayPal.me, etc.) that opens on someone else’s phone when they tap you. That is not the same as tapping a Visa terminal.

Does it track health or sleep?

No. There are no sensors and no battery. It is not a health tracker.

Will it open my office or apartment door?

Only if your access system accepts a standard NFC credential and your administrator is willing to enroll the ring. Many modern systems can; many older proprietary ones cannot. Check with your building first.

Does it need charging?

Never. The chip is passive and draws power from the phone reading it.

What phones work?

Any iPhone 7 or newer and virtually all Android phones with NFC. The other person does not need an app installed.
