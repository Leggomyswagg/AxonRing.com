Frequently asked

Does the person I tap need an app?

Not for a normal public URL or supported NDEF record. Their phone must have NFC enabled and support the saved record type.

Can I store a Wi-Fi password?

Some Android devices support NFC Wi-Fi records; iPhone behavior is more limited. Treat any unlocked NFC content as readable by anyone who scans it, so use a separate guest network and rotate its password.

Can I clone an access badge?

AxonRing does not promise cloning. Many systems use proprietary frequencies, secure credentials, rolling codes, or administrator enrollment. Ask the system owner whether a standard 13.56 MHz NFC Forum Type 2 credential is supported.

Where is the sensor-ring specification?

It is intentionally separate. AxonRing Sense will not receive a product specification or sales page until there is a working PPG + temperature prototype, published accuracy results, and an app.
