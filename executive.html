The everyday AxonRing

The Executive is the clean, daily-wear version of AxonRing. It looks like a modern matte ring. Inside is a sealed passive NFC chip.

When someone taps their phone to it, your digital business card or chosen link appears instantly — no app required on their side. Tap your own phone and it can silence notifications, start a timer, open an app, or run a routine.

It does not track your health. It does not replace your credit card at payment terminals. It removes friction from the small interactions you do dozens of times a week — without another device to charge.

Who it’s for

People who want a refined ring that actually does something useful — without the battery anxiety or subscription of a health tracker, and without the overpromising of most “smart” rings.

Important: AxonRing does not support contactless card payments at Visa or Mastercard terminals and does not track health metrics. It is a premium passive NFC ring for sharing, access (where supported), and phone automations.
