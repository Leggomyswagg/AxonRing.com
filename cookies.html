Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Short version

This site uses almost no cookies — and none for advertising.

What we use

  • Vercel Analytics — anonymous, cookieless page-view analytics. It does not identify you, set advertising cookies, or track you across other sites.
  • Stripe — when you proceed to checkout, Stripe sets cookies strictly necessary for fraud prevention and payment processing. These appear only on Stripe's checkout pages. Details: stripe.com/cookies-policy.

What we don't use

No advertising pixels, no social media trackers, no cross-site profiling, no data brokers.

Managing cookies

Because we set no optional cookies, there's nothing to opt out of on our side. Your browser settings can block or delete cookies at any time; Stripe checkout requires its essential cookies to process payment.
