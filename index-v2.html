The value proposition

Less to carry.
Less to charge. More to do.

AxonRing is deliberately simple. It gives you a physical interface for digital actions, without adding another device that needs an app, battery, or daily attention.

01 · IDENTITY

Your digital handshake

Share your contact card, LinkedIn, portfolio, website, or another URL with a single tap.

02 · AUTOMATION

Your physical shortcut

Use phone automations to turn a tap into an action such as opening an app, starting a timer, or running a routine.

03 · FREEDOM

Nothing to recharge

The NFC tag is passive. Your phone supplies the energy needed to read it, so the ring itself never needs charging.

Built on trust, not hype. AxonRing is currently a passive NFC product. It does not contain health sensors, GPS, a battery, or a certified payment secure element. We will label future capabilities separately rather than imply they exist today.

FAQ

Before you buy.

Does AxonRing require charging?

No. It uses a passive NFC tag and has no battery.

Can AxonRing make contactless card payments?

Not currently. AxonRing can store a payment URL, but it does not contain a certified payment secure element and should not be represented as a card-payment ring.

Which phones can read it?

Compatible iPhones and Android phones with NFC can read supported NFC tags. The receiving person generally does not need an AxonRing app.

How does sizing work?

A free sizing kit ships before the ring so you can confirm the correct size. Current listed sizes are US 6–13.

What happens if I change my information?

You can erase and rewrite the NFC content with a compatible NFC writing app. The ring is designed to be reusable rather than locked to one URL.

What is the return policy?

AxonRing currently offers 30-day returns. See the Terms page for the complete conditions and warranty coverage.
